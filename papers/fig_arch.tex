\caption{A disc of radius $r$ is masked out of the spectrum and a trainable
convolutional encoder reads the result at full resolution, never through the
VAE, giving a condition $z_c$ of 4 channels at $64{\times}64$. It enters the
frozen UNet at the 16 cross-attention sites, where only $W_K$, $W_V$ and a
zero-initialised output gate are trained and $W_Q$ and $W_O$ stay frozen
(Fig.~\ref{fig:sa}). A second trainable stem, drawn here as part of the same
condition path, reads the raw condition and adds zero-initialised residuals to
each of the 12 decoder skip tensors and to the mid block; that path carries
most of the structure, and the split is measured: at $r{=}0$, SC is
\pathSkipOnlyZero{} with only the skip path active and \pathAttnOnlyZero{} with
only attention (Sec.~\ref{sec:where}). Training is $\epsilon$-prediction with
Min-SNR weighting; the loop through the frozen autoencoder is the standard
latent-diffusion one and none of it is trained.}
