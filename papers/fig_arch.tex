\caption{\textbf{(a)} A disc of radius $r$ is masked out of the spectrum and a
trainable convolutional encoder reads the result at full resolution, never
through the VAE. Its features enter the frozen UNet twice: as zero-initialised
residuals on each decoder skip tensor ($\oplus$; three resolution levels and the
mid block are drawn, 12 skip heads and one mid head in the network), and through
StandAlone attention at
the 16 cross-attention sites. Stroke weight follows the measured split: at $r{=}0$, SC is
\pathSkipOnlyZero{} with only the skip path active and \pathAttnOnlyZero{} with
only attention (Sec.~\ref{sec:where}).
\textbf{(b)} One StandAlone site. The query comes from the UNet's own latent
through the frozen $W_Q$; keys and values come from the condition through
trained $1{\times}1$ projections, unfolded so each position attends over its
$3{\times}3$ neighbourhood rather than the whole map; a $1{\times}1$ window
performs at least as well (Sec.~\ref{sec:where}). The result passes the
frozen output projection and a zero-initialised per-channel gate, then is added
both into the latent --- so the text query below sees structure --- and to what
the layer returns.}
